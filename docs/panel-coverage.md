# Panel criteria review

Reviewed November 21, 2026. This review uses the eight “What we are looking for” criteria from the final exercise. All interviews and sample records use invented details.

| Criterion | What the panel can see | What to validate with the City |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Problem definition** | Slides 2–3 explain the requested AI agent and the operational need for an actionable IT decision. The Police interview on slide 14 starts with a tool staff already use and identifies a training need. [Problem and intended outcome](project-brief.md#problem-and-intended-outcome) | Confirm what IT needs to decide on first review. Use comparable current requests to establish how often analysts have to repeat discovery. |
| **Requirements and scope** | Slide 4 shows what the exercise delivers, what the City needs to test before a pilot, and which work is outside scope, including large-scale load testing. Slide 12 sets the pilot gates. The written brief adds stakeholder questions, assumptions, priorities and acceptance checks. [Requirements and acceptance](project-brief.md#requirements-and-acceptance) · [Scope and exclusions](project-brief.md#scope-and-exclusions) | Agree on authorized teams, data boundaries, retention, accessibility and performance targets, who can pause the pilot, and what warrants expansion. |
| **The session design** | Slide 5 lists the six required checks and explains when the interview moves to Review. Slide 9 explains how Qwen helps choose follow-ups and how written rules reuse details from each answer. Slide 14 shows an earlier quote and optional question help. [Slide 5 talking points](../panel/live-demo.md#how-we-know-we-have-enough) · [Interview logic](../navigator/interview.py) · [Question bank](../navigator/questions.py) | Test how staff in all three departments describe the work, including uncommon phrasing, incomplete answers, shift interruptions and use of assistive technology. |
| **The output artifact** | Slide 6 shows the report structure. Slide 7 shows the suggested owner and first action for each outcome. Slide 15 shows the Police room calendar assessment, and slide 17 compares it with the seventeen-question Fire badge case and its hold. [Police room calendar assessment](../examples/police-room-calendar-assessment.md) · [Fire badge assessment](../examples/fire-badge-access-assessment.md) | Have a designated analyst work through the report and verify that it gives them enough evidence to route the request. Confirm City role names and the manual handoff to existing intake. |
| **Recommendation quality** | Slides 7 and 16 show six outcomes and the first action each supports. The Police training-forms case defers a build while IT confirms the source and owner. The Fire badge case fits Automation and remains on hold for private staff data and physical access consequences. [Saved case assessments](../panel/demo-cases.md) · [Decision tests](../tests/test_interview.py) | Review a sample of routing decisions and overrides with analysts. Calibrate the rules and screening points against confirmed workflows and compare trade-offs with City-approved tools. |
| **Technical execution** | Slides 8–11 show the design, stack, Docker and offline deployments, local AI and data handling. Slide 12 calls for testing concurrent use and a backup restore. Slide 20 covers failure cases, including repeated actions and interrupted work. [Architecture](architecture.md) · [Verification](verification.md) | The demo runs on one host with one shared analyst account. Prove representative multi-user use, City sign-in and audit, access controls, retention, and a witnessed restore before relying on it with real data. |
| **Communication** | Slide 3 starts with the staff homepage. Slide 14 shows plain-language choices and help. The 20-minute script uses the Police case to connect staff answers to a clear next step and names the pilot’s open risks. [Demo script](../panel/live-demo.md) | Rehearse with a department lead and frontline staff. Ask them to explain the result and the next step in their own words. |
| **Ownership** | Slide 12 sets out the team pilot, validation measures and operating roles. The department owns the work, Business Systems maintains the rules, Service Desk handles first response, Platform Ops owns recovery, and City governance teams own approvals. [Phased delivery and ownership](project-brief.md#phased-delivery-and-ownership) | Secure named owners, agree on a review cadence and confirm who can pause intake. Rehearse manual fallback and review overrides before rollout expands. |
