Analyst dashboard quickstart

Find a completed assessment, check the evidence, and decide who should follow up.

Before you begin · Use the analyst credentials provided for the hosted demo. Ask the presenter if you need access.

  1. Open the portal and sign in

    From the Flagstaff WIP homepage, choose Analyst dashboard near the bottom, or open the analyst sign-in page directly. Enter your username and password.

  2. Find an assessment

    Enter the assessment ID from a staff receipt and choose Find assessment. For a wider review, filter by department, impact if wrong, or IT review status, then open an item under Completed submissions.

  3. Use the charts to prioritize

    Compare expected benefit with estimated delivery effort. Hover over a count for details, or select it to filter the list. For the Fire badge example, select High in the Automation row of Solution by impact if wrong.

  4. Check the evidence and record the next step

    Start with Request at a glance, then check the staff answers. After triage, save the IT review status. Use Draft follow-up email if you need more detail and a contact is on file.

If there’s a governance hold · Read First action and expand Governance checks from the assessment. Confirm the required approvals before any pilot with real data.
